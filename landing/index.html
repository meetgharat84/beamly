Key Features

Engineered for Pure Sound.

Beamly provides a native, desktop-grade YouTube Music experience free of bloat, trackers, and intrusions.

Ad-Free Stream Proxy

Custom in-memory streaming proxy intercepts incoming media streams and automatically filters out audio, video, and sponsor interruptions before playback triggers.

  • Zero Interruptions
  • Lossless Bitrate
  • No Video Ads

Google Playlist Sync

Seamlessly connect your Google account to synchronize your favorite YouTube Music playlists, artist subscriptions, and custom collections with zero friction.

  • Instant 1-Click Sync
  • Liked Songs Library
  • Local Cache Support

Synchronized Live Lyrics

Follow along effortlessly with real-time, line-by-line synchronized lyrics parsed with millisecond precision from community databases for sing-along sessions.

  • Line-by-Line Timing
  • Auto-Scrolling
  • Global Database
